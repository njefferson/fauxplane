PANEL POWER

This panel runs on your device's own sensors and on live aviation feeds. Nothing is simulated: if a reading is missing, the instrument shows a failure flag and the BITE page explains why.

Switching on asks for motion, orientation and location access. Everything stays on the device — no account, no analytics, nothing sent anywhere but the weather and traffic requests the panel makes on your behalf.

You can decline and still use the panel. Every instrument will show its failure flag, which is exactly what it is supposed to do.

First time here

These instructions stay in the i menu at the top of the screen after you switch on, along with what changed in each release and where the numbers come from.

It is a glass cockpit panel for a phone or tablet — the instruments an airliner shows its pilots, driven by your device's own sensors and by live aviation data. It is a toy for people who love aeroplanes. It is not a simulator, it is not certified for anything, and it is never for navigation.

PFD
The horizon, altitude, heading and G, with a plan view of real aircraft beside it.
ATIS
The weather at the nearest reporting airport, as the report actually reads.
RADAR
Real aircraft overhead. Tap one and the panel flies as that aircraft.
BITE
Built-in test: every sensor and feed, working or not, and why.
SETUP
Level the horizon to whatever the device is clamped in.

Clamp it somewhere, press Level the horizon on the PFD, and open RADAR to see what is flying near you.

Add it to your home screen

It then opens full screen with no browser bars, and works with no signal — the instruments that run on the device's own sensors need nothing from the network.

iPhone or iPad: the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
Android: the browser's menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
